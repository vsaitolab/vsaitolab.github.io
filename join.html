Join the lab

Come study freshwater ecosystems with us

We are always interested in curious, motivated people who want to understand how freshwater communities respond to global change, in the field, in the lab and in the computer.

Opportunities

Where you could fit

Undergraduate

Research initiation

Hands-on projects in field sampling, lab work and data analysis, often supported by scholarships such as FAPESP IC or CNPq PIBIC.

Graduate

Master's and PhD

I supervise students in two graduate programs at UFSCar: Environmental Sciences (PPGCAm) and Ecology and Natural Resources (PPGERN).

Postdoctoral

Postdocs

We welcome postdoctoral researchers interested in food webs, size structure and global change, for example through FAPESP postdoctoral fellowships.

Get in touch

How to start

  1. Write me an email at vssaito@ufscar.br with a short note on what interests you and why this lab.
  2. Attach your CV and, if you have one, a link to your Lattes or a sample of your work.
  3. Tell me about timing: when you would like to start and any funding calls you have in mind.
